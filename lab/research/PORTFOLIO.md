# Portfolio entry (draft for the site)

**Title:** Does learning hide a predator from evolution?

**One line:** A pre-registered test in a small artificial-life world, run only after three rounds of independent audit caught a wrong headline, four measurement bugs and a false anecdote in the first version.

**Blurb (about 120 words):**

I built a small deterministic world where creatures learn from experience and from watching each other, breed with inherited traits, and keep a fire alive across generations. I asked whether a predator selects for caution, and whether learning about the predator hides that pressure from evolution. My first version claimed it did; an independent reviewer showed the claim was written after a failed prediction and rested on buggy measurements. Three review rounds later, a fresh 256-seed pre-registered run did not support the shielding prediction: without risk learning we did not establish a positive predator-related advantage for caution, and risk learning shifted selection slightly toward boldness. Watching others roughly doubled the chance of taking up the fire. The code was written by an AI agent under my direction; the decisions, and the record of every mistake, are mine.
