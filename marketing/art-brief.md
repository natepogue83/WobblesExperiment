# Wobblins: art brief

A one-page brief for whoever paints the game, a person or a style-locked image model. Everything the game needs is listed, with sizes and what has to stay flexible. The goal is one artist's hand over the whole screen: creatures, valley, objects and menus drawn in the same breath.

## The game in two lines

You are a small god of a valley. Three soft creatures called Wobblins wake up in it, and over days and weeks they work out fire, huts, songs and a temple on their own. You never build; you drop stones, rain, seeds and lightning, pick the Wobblins up, fling them about, and watch what they make of it. Cozy, funny, a little mischievous. The player should want to look at it the way you want to look at a picture book.

## Tone and references

- **Warm, hand-painted, storybook.** Think *Cozy Grove* (watercolour, coloring-book lines), *Spiritfarer* (woodblock and watercolour, paper texture), *Botanicula* (textured, tactile, a bit odd), the chunky wobbly line and bean bodies of *Cult of the Lamb* without the darkness.
- **Not:** glossy vector, gradients that look lit by a lamp, "mobile casual" plastic shine, stock icon packs, pixel art.
- **One light** from the upper left across everything. Paper or gouache texture visible up close. Line weight varies (thicker at the bottom and in shadow, thin where light hits).
- **Palette:** muted and warm. Grass is olive and moss, not neon. Sky is milky. Creatures are the most saturated things on screen, so the eye goes to them; the backdrop stays quieter and lower in contrast than anything the player can touch.
- **Wobblins are not** egg-shaped roly-poly toys (Weebles) or yellow jelly people. Do not use or imitate the look of the Netflix *Thronglets*.

## The Wobblin

A wide, soft, squishy creature that sags a little at the base, like a bean bag with a face. A leaf sprout on top (its colour shows mood). Big eyes, blush cheeks, stubby arms and feet. About as wide as it is tall. It wobbles when it walks and squashes when it lands.

Every Wobblin is different, so the body is a kit:

- **Coat:** 10 colours (sage, moss, butter, apricot, dusty rose, lavender, slate blue, sea green, biscuit, pale olive). Needs a light side and a painted shadow side.
- **Markings:** plain, spots, two stripes, freckles, one patch over an eye.
- **Ears:** little nubs, droopy, pointy, tufts, none. **Tails:** none, stub, curl, fluff.
- **Builds:** round, tall, wide, stocky (small scale differences).
- **Ages:** baby (two thirds size, bigger eyes), adult, elder (a cane, a white brow).

**Faces are drawn by the game, not painted**, so the eyes can follow the player's hand and blink. The painter supplies the body, ears, tail, arms, feet and sprout as separate layers; the game places eyes, brows, mouth, blush and accessories on top. Eight moods: happy, content, meh, sad, scared, cold, in love, asleep.

**Accessories** (painted, placed by the game): flower crown with a butterfly, frizzy fur after lightning, a knitted hat, a big leaf held as an umbrella, paint splats, river drips, eight keepsake charms (feather, shell, moonstone, flint, bellflower, pelt, seed pouch, firefly jar), a torch, a stone tool, a woven basket.

Render size: the body is about 200 px tall at 2x, so paint around 400 px tall with a 100 px margin, PNG with alpha, feet on a fixed baseline.

## The valley

One painting, 1200 x 675 units (paint at 2400 x 1350). Fixed layout, the player must recognise it every time:

- Far: soft alpine mountains, milky sky, slow clouds.
- A hill on the left with a small stone shrine. A grove of trees on the right.
- A meadow in the middle, a hollow at lower left (where huts go), a pond site at lower right.
- A river across the bottom, a riverbank in front of it, tall grass framing the bottom edge.

Needed as separate layers: sky, mountains, far trees, ground, river, foreground grass. Plus colour variants the game blends: dusk, night, dawn, a snowy cold snap, wet ground. Dirt paths wear in where the Wobblins walk; the game draws those.

## Objects (each a PNG with alpha, painted at 2x, lit from the upper left)

Nature: berry bush (with and without berries), tree, sprout, reeds, clay patch, boulder, stones in a pile of 1-4, fish, snowman, a scorched patch, a ruin.
Built by Wobblins: hut (with a lit doorway and chimney smoke), wall, hearth, fire, garden plot, totem, temple (stacked stones, carved posts, leaf thatch, prayer flags), mural slab with handprints, raft, grave.
Keeper things: gift box, curio (a shiny oddity), the shrine, a pond.

Objects are 60-140 px wide on screen at 1x; paint at 2x.

## Menus and UI

Paper and ink. Hand-lettered titles. Needed: a paper panel frame (9-slice), a wooden tray shelf, 14 tray tiles (stones, boulder, berry seed, tree seed, rain, lightning, water, shake, cold snap, warm spell, bless, curio, daily gift, miracle), 30 small discovery icons (fire, hut, song, temple...), a hand cursor (open and closed), thought-bubble shapes (idea, question, heart, music note, smoke, zzz).

## Delivery

Phase 1, a style test (a week): one Wobblin body kit in two coats with one accessory, one hut, one tree, one slice of the valley at full size, one paper panel. We put it in the game and look at it moving before anything else is painted.
Phase 2: the rest, in the order above.

Files: layered source (PSD, Procreate or Krita) plus flattened PNGs. Consistent naming, baseline marked. Colours as a shared palette swatch.
